## 三、翻译

### 参考译文

我可以从过去的53年中随便挑一天，并立刻知道当时我在哪里、新闻里发生了什么，甚至那天是星期几。从四岁起我就具备了这个能力。

我从不因大脑吸收的信息量而感到不知所措。我的大脑似乎能够应对这些信息，并能将它们整齐地储存起来。当我想起一段悲伤的记忆时，我的做法和所有人一样——试着把它放到一边。我不认为仅仅因为我的记忆更清晰，处理悲伤记忆就会更难。强大的记忆并不会让我的情绪变得更加敏锐或生动。我能回忆起祖父去世的那一天，以及前一天我们去医院时所感到的悲伤。我还记得同一天音乐剧《长发》（Hair）在百老汇首演——这两件事都以同样的方式突然浮现在我的脑海中。

## 四、写作

### Part A 参考范文

Dear classmates,

I am writing to inform you that our class is going to hold a charity sale to help kids in need.

The charity sale will be held at the school auditorium from 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. on January 15th. We will sell second-hand books, clothes, toys and some other daily necessities. All the money raised will be donated to a charity organization and used to buy school supplies and warm clothes for children in poor areas.

I sincerely hope that all of you will take an active part in this meaningful activity. Your participation will bring warmth and hope to those kids. Please come and join us, and let's make a difference together.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

### Part B 参考范文

The bar chart above clearly presents the percentage of students taking part-time jobs in a certain university. As is shown in the chart, the proportion increased slightly from 67.77% in the freshman year to 71.13% in the sophomore year and 71.93% in the junior year. However, it jumped dramatically to 88.24% in the senior year.

Several factors may account for this trend. To begin with, freshmen have to adapt themselves to the new campus life and focus on their studies, so relatively fewer of them take part-time jobs. As time goes by, students become more familiar with the campus and have more spare time, so the proportion rises steadily. What's more, as graduation approaches, seniors are increasingly aware that work experience is essential for their future careers, so a great many of them take part-time jobs to enhance their competitiveness in the job market. In addition, taking part-time jobs can help students put what they have learned into practice and relieve their families' financial burden.

From my perspective, it is beneficial for college students to take part-time jobs as long as they keep a proper balance between study and work.

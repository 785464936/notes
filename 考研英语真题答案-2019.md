## 三、翻译

对于英国作家詹姆斯·赫里奥特，人们往往不甚重视。他的创作风格明快自如、朗朗上口，以至于令人感觉极易模仿。我三番五次地听人说："我也能写书，只是没时间。"这说来容易做来难。然而与大众观点相反，正如詹姆斯·赫里奥特所言，"尝试写作"并非易事。尽管詹姆斯·赫里奥特天赋秉异，他呈现给世人的精雕细琢的作品也皆是数年练习、重写和阅读之后才得以形成的。和大多数作家一样，他在创作之路上也屡屡经历失意和拒绝，但这一切更令他义无反顾地走向成功。他的一生所获皆是靠自己的艰苦努力所取得的，他在文学领域的成就也不例外。

## 四、写作

### Part A 应用文

Dear Prof. Smith,

It's my pleasure to plan the debate on city traffic, and I am writing mainly to put forward advice on the topic of this debate and introduce my preliminary arrangements concerned.

To begin with, the debate can be conducted with the topic of "By Bus or By Bike" on the ground that a host of residents prefer convenient vehicles to reduce time cost under the background of increasingly heavy city traffic. Therefore, the participants in this debate can take an active part in it due to their real experience in daily life. Regarding the arrangements of the debate, it will be held in the auditorium on our campus on Dec. 28th, 2018. Anyone interested in this debate can apply for participation at the Students' Union Office before Dec. 24th. Finally, a watch will be awarded to the winner of the debate.

It will be highly appreciated if you could give me an early reply or some comments on my plan.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

### Part B 图表作文

This bar chart above looks into the choices that concern graduates most when they settle down after schooling. With the staggering economy, most graduates choose to find a job after they leave school, even though there is a decline of about 8 percentage points in 2018, comparing that in 2013. The least part of them choose to establish their own business after graduation, with 1.3 percent in 2013 and 2.6 percent in 2018. As to the students who ask for further study, it accounts for 26.3 percent in 2013 and 34 percent in 2018. Besides, we can see from the chart that more students choose to continue to study instead of working after education, comparing those in 2013.

This reflects a change of attitudes of graduates towards life after schooling. I can find no better reasons for this phenomenon other than the following two. On the one hand, most graduates want to find a break after four-year study at school. This indicates that youth today are eager to face challenges and want to put what they have learned into practice. On the other hand, students are facing an ever intense competition owing to the expansion of enrollment. Most students have to do their further study. Hardly can such students find favorable jobs.

It is high time that we took effective measures to solve the problem. For one thing, it is essential that proper policies be worked out to promote students to run their own business. For another, students should be educated to get well-prepared for their future work. Only with this measure can our society improve for a sound development.

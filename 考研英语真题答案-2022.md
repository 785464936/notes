## 三、翻译

### 参考译文

有时候，即便我们竭尽全力，我们的画作也极少呈现出我们预期的效果。光线的变化，绘画材料的限制，以及经验和技巧的缺乏，这些都意味着你最初想要达到的画作效果可能不会以你所期待的方式呈现出来。虽然这可能会令人沮丧和失望，但事实证明这实际上对你有好处。得非所愿有两个好处：你很快就能学会处理失望，并意识到当一扇门关闭时，另一扇门就会打开。你也会很快学会适应，并想出创造性的解决方案去解决绘画中出现的问题，而跳出固有的思维模式将成为你的第二天性。事实上，富有创造性的解决问题的技能在日常生活中非常有用，有了这种技能，当问题出现时，你更有可能找到解决方案。

## 四、写作

### Part A 参考范文

Dear international students,

The campus food festival will be held on January 12th, 2022 in the second dining hall of our university. As an important part of cultural exchange, the food festival is intended to enrich campus life and promote communication among students.

In the festival, you will enjoy numerous traditional Chinese food and snacks of different tastes, including hot pot, dumplings, roast duck, and so on. And you will have chances to cook Chinese food by yourself. By tasting and cooking such delicious Chinese food and snacks you will gain a deeper understanding of Chinese food and culture.

All the international students are welcomed to attend the food festival. Looking forward to your arrival.

Sincerely yours,  
Li Ming

### Part B 参考范文

The bar chart is about the growth of the package volume of express delivery industry between the year of 2018 and the year of 2020 in China. To be specific, the number of express packages in the rural and total amount is 12 billion and 51 billion respectively in 2018, 15 billion and 64 billion respectively in 2019, and 30 billion and 83 billion respectively in 2020.

According to the statistics, we can see the express delivery industry witnessed a dramatic growth, especially in 2020. As far as I can see, the growth in express delivery is partly due to the rapid development of the e-commerce industry and the further improved transport system, which makes it easy for both urban and rural people to shop online. And advanced technologies, such as artificial intelligence, help improve the service of the delivery industry. In addition, the outbreak of COVID-19 may also account for the increase of express delivery business, especially in 2020. During the lockdown, people had to stay at home and bought most of their necessities online.

In view of the above analysis, I believe express delivery industry will boast a greater potential and embrace a brighter future.

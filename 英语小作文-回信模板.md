## 首段客套话

### 我在干嘛

1. I am writing in response to... 
2. I am writing to confirm that...
3. I am writing to follow up on...
4. I am writing to inquire about...
5. I would like to put forward several/specific number practical suggestions.

后面可以接从句，下方例子同样

> [!e.g.] 
> i'm writing in responce to your letter in which you mentioned...

### 行

1. I would like to express my sincere gratitude for...
2. I am delighted/flattered/... to accept your invitation and would be honored to attend.

### 不行

1. Much to my regret, I am unable to...
2. Due to unforeseen circumstances, I regret that I am unable to...
3. Please accept my sincere apologies for...
4. Thank you for your time and consideration.

这部分可以多个句子组合成一个 

> [!e.g.]
>  
> In response to your recent letter inquiring about..., it is my pleasure to provide you with detailed information and relevant arrangements.

## 正文

### 并列关系

1. not only ..., but also ...
2. both ... and ...
3. as well as ...
4. range from... to ...
5. First and foremost/First of all/To begin with
6. Secondly/Furthermore/In addition/However
7. Last but not least

### 举例或解释具体内容

1. ... as example
2. exaplified by...
3. featuring, covering, including
4. boasting, bearing, possessing
5. serving as, functioning as
6. focusing on, targeting at

#### 高级句式

1. 定语从句
2. with 复合结构（更高级😋）
    - 从句是完整的句子，`<主><谓>[宾]`，下同
    - 复合结构是短语，比从句的适用范围更窄，==🔴后面千万别出现谓语（==

> [!e.g.]
> 
> with:   
> First and foremost, the exhibition will display various traditional handicrafts, ==🔵with works ranging== from paper-cutting and shadow puppetry to ceramic making.  
> which:  
> First and foremost, the exhibition will display various traditional handicrafts, ==🔵which range== from paper-cutting and shadow puppetry to ceramic making.

3. 独立主格
    - 主句引出的复数事物，each 过去分词/现在分词==🔴短语==

> [!e.g.]
> you may encounter surviving monuments, each bearing the singular character of its age.

## 结尾客套话

1. I would greatly appreciate it if you could...
2. Please do not hesitate to contact me. I hope my reply could be helpful to you
3. I look forward to hearing from you.

> [!e.g.] 
> i'd greatly appreciate it if you could provide me with further information concerning...

##### Opening a reply

Use these expressions to acknowledge the message and introduce your response.

|Expression|Meaning / Usage|
|---|---|
|Thank you for your letter/email regarding...|感谢您关于……的来信|
|I am writing in response to your email concerning...|我写信回复您关于……的邮件|
|I am writing to respond to your inquiry about...|我写信答复您关于……的询问|
|Thank you for getting in touch with me.|感谢您与我联系|
|I appreciate your prompt response.|感谢您及时回复|
|It was a pleasure to hear from you.|很高兴收到您的来信|
|I am grateful for your interest in...|感谢您对……的关注|

Recommended universal expression:

> Thank you for your email regarding [topic]. I am writing to provide the information you requested.

##### Accepting an invitation

|Expression|Meaning|
|---|---|
|I am delighted to accept your invitation.|我很高兴接受您的邀请|
|I would be more than happy to attend.|我非常乐意参加|
|It would be my pleasure to join you.|我很荣幸能与您一同参加|
|I am looking forward to attending the event.|我期待参加此次活动|
|I would be delighted to take part in...|我很乐意参与……|
|Please count me in.|请把我算在内（较口语）|

Formal example:

> I am delighted to accept your invitation and would be honored to attend the event. I look forward to meeting everyone there.

|Expression|Meaning|
|---|---|

##### Important distinctions: avoid unnatural expressions

Some expressions frequently appear in Chinese students' English writing but are not always idiomatic.

|Avoid / Use cautiously|Better alternative|
|---|---|
|Thanks for your invitation very much.|Thank you very much for your invitation.|
|I am looking forward to hear from you.|I am looking forward to hearing from you.|
|I hope you can understand me.|I hope you will understand my situation.|
|I am very happy to receive your letter.|I am delighted to hear from you.|
|Give you some suggestions.|Offer you some suggestions.|
|I wish you have a good time.|I hope you have a wonderful time.|
|Reply me as soon as possible.|I would appreciate your prompt reply.|
|Thanks for your kindly invitation.|Thank you for your kind invitation.|

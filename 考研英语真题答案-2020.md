## 三、翻译

### 参考译文

人生在世，不可能不经历某种失败。但失败的美好之处在于，如何看待失败完全由我们自己决定。

我们可以选择把失败看作"世界末日"，也可以把它视为一种不可思议的学习经历，而它常常正是如此。每当我们做某件事失败时，我们都可以选择去寻找注定要吸取的教训。这些教训非常重要；它们是我们成长的途径，也是我们避免重蹈覆辙的方法。失败只有在我们听之任之的时候才会阻止我们。

失败还能让我们认识那些若非如此永远不会了解的自己。例如，失败能帮助你发现自己是一个多么坚强的人。在某件事上的失败能帮助你发现最真诚的朋友，或帮助你找到意想不到的前进动力。

## 四、写作

### Part A 参考范文

Dear international students,

I am writing to tell you that I have arranged a tour to the Palace Museum for next Saturday. As one of the most famous historical sites in China, it served as the imperial palace of the Ming and Qing dynasties and now houses a vast collection of cultural treasures.

During the tour, you will be able to admire the magnificent ancient architecture and learn about the rich history and culture behind it. Here are some tips for you. First, please wear comfortable shoes and clothes, for we will do a lot of walking. Second, remember to bring some water and snacks. Third, please arrive at the school gate on time and keep together with the group.

I am sure you will have a wonderful time. Looking forward to seeing you then.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

### Part B 参考范文

The pie chart above shows the purposes of reading on mobile phones among students in a certain university. As can be seen, acquiring knowledge accounts for the largest proportion, at 59.5%, followed by killing time and browsing information, which take up 21.3% and 17.0% respectively. The rest, 2.2%, goes to other purposes.

There are several reasons behind this phenomenon. In the first place, with the popularity of smartphones and the development of mobile internet, students can easily get access to various learning resources at any time and in any place, which makes mobile reading an efficient way to study. In the second place, as college life is getting increasingly busy, many students tend to read on their phones to relax and kill time during their spare time. Last but not least, the instant access to information also attracts some students to read on their mobile phones.

In conclusion, the chart reflects the important role that mobile reading plays in students' daily lives. It is advisable for students to make good use of mobile reading to acquire knowledge and broaden their horizons.

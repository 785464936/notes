## 振荡环节

### 传递函数

$$
    G(s) = \frac{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}^{2}}{s^{2}+2\zeta \omega_{\mathrm{n}}s+\omega_{\mathrm{n}}^{2}}
$$

- 限定阻尼比 $0<\zeta<1{}$

### 幅频特性

$$
    \begin{align}
    A(\omega) & = \frac{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}^{2}}{\sqrt{(\omega_{\mathrm{n}}^{2}-\omega^{2})^{2}+(2\zeta \omega_{\mathrm{n}}\omega)^{2}}} \\
     & =\frac{1}{\sqrt{\left[ 1-\left( \dfrac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}} \right)^{2} \right]^{2}+\left( 2\zeta \dfrac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}} \right)^{2}}}
    \end{align}
$$

幅频特性与阻尼比 $\zeta{}$ 有关，$\omega:0\to \infty{}$ 对应 $A(\omega):1\to 0{}$，且

- $\omega = \omega_{\mathrm{n}}{}$ 时，$A(\omega_{\mathrm{n}}) = \dfrac{1}{2\zeta}{}$
- 阻尼比较小（$0<\zeta<0.707{}$）时，令 $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}t} = 0{}$，可求出
    - **谐振频率** $\omega_{\mathrm{r}} = \omega_{\mathrm{n}}\sqrt{1-2\zeta^{2}}{}$，代入得
    - **谐振峰值** $M_{\mathrm{r}} = \lvert G(\mathrm{j}\omega_{\mathrm{r}}) \rvert = \dfrac{1}{2\zeta \sqrt{1-\zeta^{2}}}{}$

|                                                      |                    |                                                                                                                                                                         |
|:----------------------------------------------------:|:------------------:|:-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|       $\zeta>0.707{}$<br>$(\beta<45\degree){}$       | $1-2\zeta^{2}<0{}$ |                                                              $\begin{cases}\omega_{\mathrm{r}} \\ M_{\mathrm{r}}\end{cases}{}$不存在                                                               |
|       $\zeta=0.707{}$<br>$(\beta=45\degree){}$       | $1-2\zeta^{2}=0{}$ |                                                $\begin{cases}\omega_{\mathrm{r}} = 0 \\ M_{\mathrm{r}} = 1\end{cases}{}$                                                |
| $0<\zeta<0.707{}$<br>$(45\degree<\beta<90\degree){}$ | $1-2\zeta^{2}>0{}$ | $\begin{cases}\omega_{\mathrm{r}} = \omega_{\mathrm{n}}\sqrt{1-2\zeta^{2}}<\omega_{\mathrm{n}} \\ M_{\mathrm{r}} = \dfrac{1}{2\zeta \sqrt{1-\zeta^{2}}}>1\end{cases}{}$ |
|         $\zeta=0{}$<br>$(\beta=90\degree){}$         | $1-2\zeta^{2}=1{}$ |                                    $\begin{cases}\omega_{\mathrm{r}} = \omega_{\mathrm{n}} \\ M_{\mathrm{r}} = \infty \end{cases}{}$引起共振                                    |

### 相频特性

$$
    \varphi(\omega) = \angle G(\mathrm{j}\omega) = -\arctan \dfrac{2\zeta \dfrac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}}}{1-\left( \dfrac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}} \right)^{2}} = -360\degree+\arctan \dfrac{2\zeta \dfrac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}}}{1-\left( \dfrac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}} \right)^{2}}
$$

- 虚部/实部

幅频特性与阻尼比 $\zeta{}$ 有关，$\omega:0\to \infty{}$ 对应 $A(\omega):0\to -\dfrac{\pi}{2}{}$，且振荡环节的频率特性有两个特征点

- $\omega = \omega_{\mathrm{n}}{}$ 时，$\begin{align} A(\omega_{\mathrm{n}}) = \dfrac{1}{2\zeta} \\ \varphi(\omega_{\mathrm{n}}) = -\frac{\pi}{2} \end{align}{}$
- $\omega = \omega_{\mathrm{r}}{}$ 时，$A(\omega_{\mathrm{r}}) = \dfrac{1}{2\zeta \sqrt{1-\zeta^{2}}}{}$

### 幅相特性

$$
    G(\mathrm{j}\omega) = \frac{1}
    {\sqrt{\left[ 1-\left( \dfrac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}} \right)^{2} \right]^{2}
    +     \left( 2\zeta \dfrac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}} \right)^{2}}}
    \cdot e^{-\mathrm{j} \arctan \frac{2\zeta \frac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}}}{1-\left( \frac{\omega}{\omega_{\mathrm{n}}} \right)^{2}}}
$$

- $G(\mathrm{j}0) = 1\angle 0{}$
- $G(\mathrm{j}\omega_{\mathrm{n}}) = \dfrac{1}{2\zeta}\angle -\dfrac{\pi}{2}{}$
- $G(\mathrm{j}\infty) = 0\angle -\pi{}$

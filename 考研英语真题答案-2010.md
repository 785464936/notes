## 三、翻译

### 参考译文

"可持续性"最近成了一个流行词，但对泰德·宁来说，这个概念却有着独特的个人意义。他在个人生活中经历了一段痛苦的、不可持续的时期，这让他清楚地认识到：以可持续发展为导向的价值观必须通过日常行动和选择来体现。

宁回忆起上世纪90年代末卖保险的那段迷茫的一年。他经历了互联网经济的繁荣与破灭，在急于找工作时，与博尔德一家代理公司签了约。

事情并不顺利。"那真是糟糕的一步，因为那不是我的兴趣所在，"宁说。他的纠结很自然转化成了销售业绩不佳。"我很痛苦。我焦虑不堪，常在夜半醒来，盯着天花板。我没有钱，又需要这份工作。人人都说：'再等等，你会渡过难关的，给它一点时间。'"

## 四、写作

### Part A 参考范文

Dear friend,

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to you for your warm reception during my stay in America. It was your help that made my trip fruitful and rewarding. Your hospitality and generous assistance really impressed me and my colleagues.

I really hope that you will pay a visit to China in the near future. I will show you around to appreciate the splendid Chinese culture and natural beauty. I am sure you will have a good time here.

Once again, I would like to express my appreciation for your kindness and welcome you to China in due course.

Yours sincerely,  
Zhang Wei

### Part B 参考范文

The chart above clearly shows the mobile-phone subscription growth from 1990 to 2008. According to the chart, subscriptions in developing countries grew slowly at the beginning and then experienced a dramatic increase after 2004, reaching around 4 billion in 2008. In contrast, subscriptions in developed countries kept a steady but slow increase over the period, at about 1 billion in 2008.

Several factors may account for this phenomenon. To begin with, developing countries with their large populations, especially China and India, have seen rapid economic growth in the past decade, which enables more people to afford mobile phones. Moreover, with the rapid development of telecommunication technology, the cost of mobile phones and services has declined sharply, making them accessible to ordinary people. Last but not least, mobile phones have become necessities in people's daily life for communication, entertainment and even business purposes.

From the analysis above, we can safely conclude that with the development of economy and technology, the number of mobile-phone users will continue to grow in the future, and mobile phones will play an increasingly important role in people's lives.

## 三、翻译

### 参考译文

超市旨在吸引顾客尽可能长时间地停留在店中。其原因很简单：顾客在店里停留的时间越长，看到的商品越多；而看到的商品越多，买的就会越多。超市中陈列着大量的商品。根据食品营销协会的调查，普通的超市大概有 44000 种不同的商品，还有很多超市的商品高达上万种。如此多的选择足以使顾客面对超负荷的信息。根据脑部扫描实验，需要快速做出这么多决定很快就会让我们难以承受。大约在购物 40 分钟之后，大多数人就不再努力做出理性的选择了，取而代之的是情绪化购物——而这时，我们的购物车中已经装了一半根本就没想买的东西。

## 四、写作

### Part A 参考范文

Dear Jack,

I'm writing to express my gratitude for your congratulation on my success in the translation contest.

As for my experience, I would like to offer you some suggestions. First and foremost, you need to practice with painstaking effort in this field. What's more, you had better communicate more with foreigners, which guarantees a deep understanding of foreign culture. Last but not least, you are supposed to participate in some international events to accumulate a wealth of practical experience.

Thanks again, and I do hope you could take my suggestions into consideration. I wish you good luck and great success in your future study.

Yours sincerely,  
Li Ming

### Part B 参考范文

As is clearly reflected in the above pie chart, the purposes of students travelling abroad demonstrate obvious differences in one college of China. According to the data given, the purpose of enjoying the beautiful landscape takes a comparatively large share, accounting for 37%, while that of relieving pressure also accounts for 33% of the whole proportion.

What triggers this phenomenon? It is not difficult to put forward several factors responsible for it. To start with, with the rapid economic development, people, including college students, are becoming increasingly wealthy, which enables them to afford the once-deemed-expensive overseas travelling. In order to enjoy the charming landscape all around the world, a large proportion of students choose to travel abroad. What's more, along with the ever-accelerating improvement of economy and society comes the ever-increasing work and life pressure. Consequently, the purpose of relieving pressure ranks second among all the purposes for people to travel around the world.

In view of the arguments above, we can conclude that the current phenomenon is no surprise. And therefore, it can be predicted that admiring the scenery and alleviating pressure will still be the main purposes for people to arrange travelling to other countries.
